Abstract

This dataset includes spatial transcriptomics data from cervical, thoracic, lumbar and sacral sections of the adult mouse spinal cord with retrogradely labeled ascending spinal projection neurons innervating Posterior Triangle Nucleus of the Thalamus (PoT, tdTomato), Cerebellum (CER, GFP) and Superior Colliculus (SCol, mNeonGreen). It was generated on the Xenium (10x Genomics) platform including gene expression data from 480 genes to discriminate major cell classes and neuron types in the mouse spinal cord and determine the projectome and sex specific differences of the structure. This resource was generated as part of the BRAIN Initiative Cell Atlas Network (BICAN).
