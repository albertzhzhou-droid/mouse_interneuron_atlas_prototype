This queue supersedes administrative workflow as the immediate development priority. “Complete” means the declared software increment and its evidence boundary are implemented; it does not mean the biological atlas is complete.

Global stable-key/no-join rule: every admitted or proposed record must retain its source namespace and stable deposited object, section, sample, donor, library, barcode, accession or version key. A join is allowed only when the source deposits an exact shared key for that relationship; co-display, similar labels or markers, matching region or age, computational integration and paper-level context create no same-cell, same-type, spatial, causal, morphology or connectivity join. Missing keys remain missing.
