# I48 · Additional same-record DAT / SWC candidate protocol

This protocol was saved before calculating any new candidate fit. It evaluates the six already pinned records 249676, 50424, 50394, 50408, 45674 and 108978 under UPG261; the completed 45718 transform remains independent and unchanged. It does not copy 45718 coefficients, its 0.01 µm display screen or its contour correspondence.

Every candidate must first pass its existing original DAT, full selected-file audit, admitted-coordinate and exact standardized SWC byte/hash pins. A source file header is evidence about its conversion only; source identity is the full record/release/asset key, not a shared source name. Original points, radii, raw closure flags, source order, topology anomalies, and the existing 50394 DER remain unchanged.

The bounded soma-landmark method requires one exact source-name group, planar source loops, unique source z in both representations, equal native-contour / SWC type-1 point counts, and one unique constant z translation matching the complete sets. Where the SWC header explicitly states the contour-to-point count, record that statement verbatim with its line locator and verify the count. A missing statement, unequal counts, repeated z or multiple possible translations is a held correspondence, not a statement that no other coordinate relation could ever exist.

For an eligible candidate, match only by the unique full-set source z translation. Each native landmark is the arithmetic mean of its exact original contour vertices. This is a declared project averaging hypothesis, not a source-deposited vertex-to-node identity. Preserve all original contour and SWC point IDs and raw order in the result. Native source z order must not be described as anatomical section order.

Declare two independent XY model families: proper rotation plus uniform scale and translation, and reflected rotation plus uniform scale and translation. Fit both using source-specific least squares, with a separate z translation fixed by the unique set match. Never copy another record's parameters or alter the model after seeing held-out residuals. Also calculate the alternative polygon-area centroid landmarks with both families; report their outcomes as a predeclared alternative averaging hypothesis, without replacing the primary arithmetic-mean method to obtain a preferred result.

Sort matched pairs by numeric source z for reproducible indexing. Primary fitting uses the first floor(2N/3) pairs and holds the remaining pairs out. Independent alternating sensitivity fits even indices and holds odd indices out. Require at least three training and three held-out pairs and a non-degenerate two-dimensional landmark set. Hold-out pairs must not enter their corresponding parameter fit. Report training and held-out XY residuals per axis, Euclidean maximum/RMS and actual pair IDs for every family and split, with full-precision calculations distinguished from rounded display.

Record the grid observed in the original serialized SWC XY tokens. A half-grid interval consistency check is an explicitly exploratory serialization model: it assumes nearest-grid rounding, which the deposited header does not itself prove. Compare each held-out axis residual against half the observed grid, plus a stated 1e-9 µm numerical guard. This is derived separately from this record's serialized coordinate precision; it is not the 45718 threshold, measurement accuracy, biological tolerance, section spacing calibration or a geometry admission rule. A failed check remains failed without parameter retuning. A passing check alone admits no overlay.

The audit must distinguish: byte-verified source files; uniquely matched numeric soma landmarks under this method; numerical fit / serialization hypotheses; and an independently approved display contract. This cycle admits no transformed contour, surface, membrane, volume, internal object, biological measurement, cross-record identity or registration. Even an eligible source-specific numerical relation remains a candidate until a separate source/view contract verifies its exact coordinate semantics, representation and independent runtime source authority.

Retain the method-specific held cases, original inputs and all evaluated alternatives. New evidence may reopen a held case only when its exact missing input is supplied. Do not modify the frozen 2.90 manifest or existing 45718 crosswalk.
