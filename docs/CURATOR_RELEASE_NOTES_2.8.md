# Curator release notes — candidate 2.8

**Workbench release:** `ohri-mouse-interneuron-public-data-workbench-2.8.0`  
**Curator distribution:** `2.8.0-curator.1`  
**Scope:** named private curator review only; not authorized for public deployment or public release.

Within the declared software and evidence boundaries, candidate 2.8 completes UPG-022 (reference outer/envelope anatomy), UPG-023 (type landscape and coverage matrix), and UPG-024 (source-ID internal-compartment extraction and plane-local rendering). This does not mean the biological atlas is complete.

Candidate 2.8 replaces the main procedural cord scaffold with an outer-cord surface and a coarse gray-matter row-envelope lofted from 15 checksum-locked native `allen_cord_20um` contour planes. The old row-envelope is not a full gray boundary. This is an `INF` display derivative, not a full-resolution segmentation. Measurement is forbidden, named segment assignment remains pending, and the release contains **0 registrations**. If the contour payload is unavailable or invalid, no synthetic anatomical fallback is shown.

The pinned annotation now supplies seven atlas-source-ID major structures: dorsal horn (90), central spinal cord (100), ventral horn (110), central canal (16), dorsal funiculus (74), lateral funiculus complete (75), and ventral funiculus (70). Each structure retains 15 ordered plane records. Across them, the payload preserves 408 separate 4-connected components and 408 exact mask-edge polygons. Dorsal funiculus ID 74 at z1470 is the one explicit missing plane because its source mask is absent there. The main scene renders internal anatomy only as plane-local line segments using a seven-color project display-only palette keyed to preserved source IDs. Display colors carry no source/anatomical authority; each exact source color is retained in `userData`. The renderer never lofts, fills, joins, manually bridges, or treats plane-local components as cross-plane-corresponded objects. The derivative remains `INF`, measurement-forbidden, unnamed by spinal segment, and **0 REG**.

The Type Landscape exposes 65 Russ 2021 non-motoneuron interneuron/projection-neuron candidates as `TYPE_LEVEL` and unplaced. These are not 65 confirmed interneurons. It does not create exact cross-study identities, cell coordinates, linked morphologies, or registrations. Four non-candidate neural populations remain source context within the pinned 69-population taxonomy.

A five-source by seven-axis knowledge-coverage matrix covers Russ 2021, Häring 2018, Sathyamurthy 2018, Delile 2019, and Tabulae Paralytica 2024 across taxonomy, region/lamina context, life stage, injury state, spatial measurement, causal mechanism, and morphology. Every one of the 35 source-by-axis judgments retains a publication locator, and release gates freeze seven audited locator corrections. Every cross-study mapping remains `not_established`; morphology remains `not_measured`; public metadata access does not assume asset-level reuse permission; and missing coverage is not biological absence. Tabulae alone retains a source-bounded `direct` causal entry because the primary study reports that AAV-DTR was delivered two weeks before complete crush SCI and diphtheria toxin was administered at eight weeks to ablate thoracic Vsx2ON neurons, preventing natural walking recovery (Extended Data Fig. 3j–l; Supplementary Video 2). That status is specific to the source definition, injury model, vector and toxin timing, and outcome; it does not transfer to every source label, a Russ population, or a project entity.

Reference anatomy (`INF`), molecular types (`TYPE_LEVEL`), source-local morphology (`OBS`), and SCI evidence entities/pathways (`SYN`/`SCH`) remain separate lanes. Co-display creates no same-cell, same-specimen, morphology, coordinate, lineage, or causal join. Individual Rexed laminae, named nuclei, and tract geometry remain gated on identifier-preserving extraction from a pinned annotation source rather than procedural filling.

The package retains the public-data-only boundary: no OHRI internal experimental data, physical specimens, unpublished institutional records, external acquisition, source-object retention, or public-release authorization. Curator review cannot promote a lane join or create `REG`.
