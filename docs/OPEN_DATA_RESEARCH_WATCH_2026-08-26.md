# Open-data research watch — 2026-08-26

Policy: public digital sources only. A public download link is not, by itself, permission to redistribute an asset in a packaged application. Every future adapter must pin the exact object, version, checksum, sample frame, coordinate system, and asset-level reuse terms before admission.

## Model consequences

1. No reviewed resource adds a dense synapse-resolved adult mouse spinal-interneuron connectome.
2. No reviewed resource justifies generating missing dendrites or axons. Cell boundaries, tissue imagery, density fields, projections and neurite reconstructions remain different object classes.
3. Transcriptomic clusters, developmental lineages, functional populations, projection-defined cells and injury-induced states remain different ontology axes.
4. A candidate marker relationship may enter a review queue, but exact cross-source identity remains zero until a versioned benchmark with ambiguity and negative-control handling passes.
5. Sample-cell or sample-nucleus counts describe dataset sampling. They are not biological prevalence without animal/specimen denominators and a valid sampling frame.
6. Public spatial coordinates remain source-local until specimen identity, axes, units, transforms and held-out physical-space error are pinned.

## Recommended execution order

1. Finish the two-source type-evidence dossier and comparison model.
2. Pin Cano-Gomez/BIL manifests and class-exclusion rules; admit metadata before large assets.
3. Benchmark SpinalTRAQ as a separate cervical reference space.
4. Add age-aware V1 and cholinergic source taxonomies.
5. Add condition-resolved SCI spatial context without turning spots into cells.
6. Add NWB electrophysiology after immutable-version and duplicate audits.
7. Keep morphology and connectivity admission guards ahead of every new visual layer.
